Logo Usage

Logo Variations

The Absolute Pro Plumbing logo suite features five variations, each designed for specific applications. All variations feature the Golden Gate Bridge symbolizing our Bay Area roots.

Logo 1: Simplified Bridge

Use Case:

Primary logo for most applications. Clean, simplified bridge design.

Best For:

Website headers, business cards, letterheads, social media profile images.

Logo 2: Dynamic Wave Bridge

Use Case:

Enhanced version with dynamic water wave element and water droplets.

Best For:

Marketing materials, service vehicle graphics, promotional items where extra visual interest is desired.

Logo 3: Classic Bridge

Use Case:

Traditional bridge silhouette with extended cables.

Best For:

Professional documents, formal communications, official letterheads.

Logo 4: Bridge with Water Waves

Use Case:

Bridge with decorative water waves beneath in navy blue.

Best For:

Secondary marketing materials, email signatures, presentations.

Logo 5: Extended Bridge with Waves

Use Case:

Full-width bridge design with water waves below.

Best For:

Banners, website headers, large format prints, signage where horizontal space is available.

Logo Specifications

Colors: Bridge in Burnt Orange (#E76F51), waves in Warm Navy (#1E4A6A)

Typography: Montserrat Bold for 'ABSOLUTE' and 'PRO', Montserrat Regular for 'PLUMBING INC.'

Minimum Size: 2 inches wide for print, 200px wide for digital

Clear Space: Maintain clear space equal to the height of 'PRO' around all sides

Do's and Don'ts

DO:

  • Use the logo on cream, white, or very light backgrounds
  • Maintain proper clear space around the logo
  • Use high-resolution files for all applications
  • Choose the logo variation that best fits your application

DON'T:

  • Stretch, distort, or alter the proportions
  • Change the logo colors
  • Add effects like drop shadows or gradients
  • Place on busy or low-contrast backgrounds
  • Mix elements from different logo variations

When to Use Each Font

Montserrat (Sans-Serif)

  • Headlines and section titles
  • Navigation menus
  • Buttons and CTAs
  • Body copy and descriptions
  • Service names and features
  • Pricing and numbers
  • Footer content

Merriweather (Serif)

  • Customer testimonials
  • Review quotes
  • Customer stories
  • Pullquotes from clients
  • Featured feedback
  • Case study testimonials

Type Scale

H1: Main Headlines (36-48pt)
H2: Section Headers (28-32pt)
H3: Subsections (22-24pt)
Body Text: Standard paragraph text uses Montserrat Regular at 16-18pt for optimal readability across all devices and mediums.

Font Weights

Bold (700): Main headlines, logo text, important CTAs

SemiBold (600): Subheadings, emphasized text, navigation

Medium (500): Buttons, links, slight emphasis

Regular (400): Body copy, paragraphs, standard text

Best Practices

  • Use line-height of 1.5-1.6 for body text to ensure readability
  • Keep paragraph widths between 50-75 characters for optimal reading
  • Use SemiBold for emphasis, not underlines or italics
  • Maintain consistent letter-spacing (tracking) at default settings
  • Use Merriweather in italic style for testimonials to add personal warmth

Web Implementation

Google Fonts import:

<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat:wght@400;500;600;700&family=Merriweather:ital,wght@0,400;0,700;1,400&display=swap" rel="stylesheet"> <link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat:wght@400;500;600;700&family=Merriweather:ital,wght@0,400;0,700;1,400&display=swap" rel="stylesheet">

Why This Pairing Works

Visual Hierarchy: The contrast between geometric sans-serif (Montserrat) and humanist serif (Merriweather) creates clear distinction between brand voice and customer voice.

Emotional Impact: Serif fonts feel more personal and handwritten, making testimonials feel more authentic and trustworthy.

Professional + Warm: Together, they balance professionalism with approachability—exactly your brand personality.

Voice & Tone

Our Voice

We speak like a trusted neighbor who happens to be an expert plumber. Professional but never stuffy, knowledgeable but never condescending, efficient but never rushed.

Voice Characteristics

Warm & Friendly

"We're here to help" not "Contact us for service"

Clear & Direct

"We'll fix your leak fast" not "We provide expedited leak mitigation services"

Reassuring

"Don't worry, we've got this" not "Plumbing problems require immediate professional attention"

Local

"Serving the Bay Area since 2005" not "Regional service provider"

Messaging Examples

Website Headlines

✓ DO:

"Fast, Reliable Plumbing When You Need It Most"

✗ DON'T:

"Comprehensive Plumbing Solutions Provider"

Emergency Messaging

✓ DO:

"Pipe burst? We'll be there in 30 minutes or less."

✗ DON'T:

"24/7 emergency response capabilities available"
